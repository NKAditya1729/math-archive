# Mathematical Lecture Archive

Line-by-line reading notes corresponding directly to recorded mathematics lecture courses. Each page is a faithful written transcript of the lecture's own order, definitions, examples, and proofs, supplemented with redrawn board diagrams and explanatory notes.

---

## Courses

### [Point-Set Topology]({{ site.baseurl }}/point-set-topology/)
**40 lectures, complete.** A foundational course in general topology covering topological spaces, bases, subspace and product topologies, metric spaces, connectedness, compactness, local compactness, one-point compactifications, quotient spaces, topological groups, separation axioms, Urysohn's lemma, Tietze extension theorem, and Urysohn metrization.
